## 🇺🇸 United States
US stock futures were mixed before the open. Dow futures rose on strong bank earnings. Nasdaq futures fell on worries about memory chips (chips that store data) and the AI trade. The last session ended mixed, with the Dow up 0.38% and the S&P 500 down 0.06%.

- **Goldman Sachs profits surge 29%.** Strong trading revenue drove the jump.
- **JPMorgan profits rise.** CEO Jamie Dimon warned that economic forces "shift under the surface."
- **Cracks grow across the AI trade.** Memory-chip stocks are tumbling.

## 🇪🇺 Europe
European shares were mostly higher at midday. The FTSE 100 led. The EURO STOXX 50 slipped 0.10%. Banks, lower oil and lower bond yields (the return on government loans) helped. Hopes for a Russia-Ukraine energy truce also helped.

- **European shares climb on bank gains.** Investors now look ahead to earnings season (the period when companies report profits).
- **LVMH drags luxury stocks.** The broad market still hit a one-week high.
- **VW CEO says mass-market carmakers are under "enormous pressure."**

## 🌏 Asia

- **Chipmaking outlook, oil and bond yields rattle Asian stocks.** The Nikkei and KOSPI were named.
