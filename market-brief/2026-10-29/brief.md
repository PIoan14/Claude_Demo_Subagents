# Market Brief — 2026-10-29 01:05 EET

## At a glance
- The US Federal Reserve (the US central bank) kept interest rates the same. The vote was split, because inflation (rising prices) is still stubborn.
- US stocks closed a little higher. The S&P 500 rose +0.28%.

- **SK Hynix says memory is sold out through 2029.** This pushed the KOSPI above 6,000 for the first time. [link](https://coindcx.com/blog/us-stock/sk-hynix-sells-out-memory-till-2029-kospi-crosses-6000)
