## 🇺🇸 United States
US futures point to a lower open. The drop comes from Alphabet's record one-day loss and an Amazon earnings miss. These revived worries about how much companies are spending on AI. The VIX (a gauge of expected market swings, often called the "fear index") rose 7.63% to 18.51. The 10-year Treasury yield (the interest rate on US government debt) eased to 4.9890.

- **Alphabet loses $482B in its worst day since its 2004 IPO.** It is the largest one-day loss in the company's history. [link](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/alphabet-heads-for-a-500-billion-drop-in-market-value-the-biggest-in-its-history-134824763.html)
- **Amazon slides after a Q3 earnings miss.** Third-quarter results came in below expectations. [link](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/live/amazon-earnings-live-q3-earnings-miss-sends-stock-lower-203345870.html)
- **US GDP growth (the size of the economy) accelerated in Q3 and beat expectations.** This points to a resilient economy. [link](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/articles/us-gdp-growth-accelerated-third-131135158.html)

## 🇪🇺 Europe
European stocks ended Oct 29 broadly higher. The DAX (Germany) rose 1.31%, the CAC 40 (France) rose 1.05% and the EURO STOXX 50 rose 0.64%. The FTSE 100 (UK) shows no change, which looks unreliable, so treat it as unverified. These are Oct 29 closes, not Oct 30 trading. I found no news that explains the moves.

- **European stocks gained at the start of the week (Oct 26).** The STOXX 600 rose about 0.8% and the STOXX 50 about 0.69%. This is the only recent European item found, and it is older than the data above. [link](https://gbm.com/media/noticia/el-indice-stoxx-600-cierra-con-un-alza-del-08/)

## 🌏 Asia
Asia was mostly lower, led by tech-heavy North Asia. KOSPI fell 3.48%, the Nikkei fell 2.12% and the Shanghai Composite fell 1.44%. Hang Seng was the exception, up 1.55%. The cause was a global tech and AI sell-off.

- **Japan's Nikkei slumps as tech stocks tumble on AI worries.** Investors dumped technology shares. [link](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/world-indices/articles/japans-nikkei-slumps-3-tech-022028372.html)
- **Stocks sink, bonds leap as AI giants' spending spooks investors.** Worries about AI spending hit shares globally, while bonds rallied. [link](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stocks-slump-bonds-leap-ai-024810978.html)
- **SK Hynix, Nvidia and Micron are off their highs.** Memory and AI chip leaders have pulled back from their peaks. [link](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/sk-hynix-nvidia-and-micron-send-one-clear-message-to-investors-with-their-stocks-off-the-highs-093713505.html)

## What to watch
- **AI spending worries.** Alphabet, Amazon and Asian chip stocks all point to doubts about big AI budgets. Watch whether the sell-off spreads to Europe.
- **Oil and Iran.** Crude rose on signs the US could renew strikes on Iran. Further moves could push energy prices higher.
- **Data quality gaps.** Europe's figures are a day old and the Asia news is partly inferred. Check live quotes before acting on them.
